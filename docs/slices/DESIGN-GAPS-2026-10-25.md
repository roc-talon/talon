# Design gaps of PLAN-P1 section 5b: decisions (2026-10-25)

The design agent went through the design gap rows of `PLAN-P1.md` 5b, one gap at a time. Method: read the slice section, prefer the reading that the code already follows when it is sane, prefer the simple and safe choice (Cargo, rustup, npm, Bun where the design already refers to them), edit the design text, write one row here. A real product choice goes to "For the user" with options and a recommendation.

The user answers 1 to 4 of 2026-10-25 (Part A) are in `PLAN-P1.md` 5b and 2c: R-AL10 to R-AL12 and the Resolution 5 example (answer 1), R-SO14 and R-SO15 (answer 2), R-IS6 and R-S4 (answer 3), R-D2, R-GC5, R-CC9, R-GC14, R-RR (answer 4).
